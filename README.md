# Waste Management Awareness in Rural Areas

## About the Project
Waste Management Awareness in Rural Areas is a project designed to create awareness among village people about proper waste disposal and maintaining a clean environment. It explains how waste segregation, recycling, and composting can help reduce pollution and improve public health.

## Objectives
- To create awareness about waste management in rural areas.
- To explain the importance of separating wet and dry waste.
- To encourage recycling and reuse of materials.
- To promote composting of biodegradable waste.
- To maintain cleanliness and protect the environment.

## Types of Waste
- **Wet Waste:** Vegetable peels, fruit waste, and food leftovers.
- **Dry Waste:** Paper, plastic, glass, and cardboard.
- **Hazardous Waste:** Batteries, chemicals, and other harmful materials.

## Activities Performed
- Conducting awareness programs in village areas.
- Explaining wet and dry waste segregation.
- Encouraging recycling and composting.
- Sharing information about the harmful effects of improper waste disposal.
- Promoting cleanliness and environmental protection.

## Benefits
- Reduces environmental pollution.
- Improves village cleanliness.
- Helps prevent the spread of diseases.
- Encourages recycling and reuse.
- Supports a healthy and sustainable environment.
